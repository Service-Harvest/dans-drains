What a Camera Inspection Shows

A sewer camera sends a live video feed through your line so we can see the actual condition inside: a clog, a cracked or collapsed section, root intrusion, or a low spot holding water. No guessing, no exploratory digging.

It turns a mystery backup into a clear picture of what needs to happen.

When to Get an Inspection

An inspection is worth it when drains repeatedly back up, before buying a home, or after a big clog to confirm the line is sound. It is also the smart first step before any sewer line repair, so the fix targets the real problem.

For a home purchase especially, it can reveal an expensive issue before you own it.

How the Inspection Works

We feed a waterproof camera on a flexible line into the sewer and watch the feed as it travels through the pipe. We can locate a problem's exact position and depth, which makes any repair precise.

You get a clear explanation of what we found and what your options are.

Inspecting Older Westchester Sewers

Many established neighborhoods here have older clay or cast-iron sewer lines that are prone to cracks and root intrusion. A camera inspection is the reliable way to know their true condition.

Because sewer integrity matters for public health, in line with EPA wastewater guidance, it is worth knowing what shape your line is in.

When to Call a Pro

Call us when drains keep backing up, before you buy a home, or when you want to confirm a line is sound after a clog. Knowing the real condition prevents wasted money on the wrong fix.

If the camera reveals heavy buildup rather than damage, a thorough jetting may be all you need.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why get a sewer camera inspection?

It shows the exact condition inside your line — clog, crack, roots, or a low spot — so any repair is targeted rather than guesswork. It also helps before buying a home.

Should I inspect the sewer before buying a house?

It is a smart move, especially with older homes. A camera can reveal a costly sewer problem before you own it, which is far better than discovering it later.

Can the camera find exactly where the problem is?

Yes. We can locate the position and depth of an issue, which makes any repair precise and avoids unnecessary digging.

Do I need an inspection or just a cleaning?

If a drain clogs once, cleaning may be enough. If it keeps backing up, an inspection tells us why so we can solve it for good.

What are the most common things you find?

In this area, root intrusion and cracked or aged clay and cast-iron pipe are the most common findings, along with grease buildup.
