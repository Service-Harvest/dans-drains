When a Home Needs Repiping

Repiping makes sense when leaks keep popping up in new spots, water pressure has dropped across the house, or the home still has aging pipe like galvanized steel that is corroding throughout.

If only part of the system is failing, a targeted replacement of that pipe run may be enough — we will tell you honestly which fits.

How We Repipe a Home

We plan the project to minimize disruption: mapping the runs, staging the work, and keeping wall openings as small as possible. We replace the old pipe with modern material and solid connections, then test everything before closing up.

Good planning is what separates a smooth repipe from a chaotic one, and we treat it as a project, not a scramble.

What Affects Repiping Cost

Repiping pricing depends on:

  • The size of the home and number of fixtures.
  • How accessible the existing pipe is.
  • The pipe material used.
  • Any wall and finish repair afterward.

We provide a clear, upfront price for the whole project.

Repiping Older Westchester Homes

Plenty of homes in this area are old enough to have original or early-replacement pipe that is now corroding. Repiping restores pressure and reliability and ends the cycle of surprise leaks.

Work follows plumbing code, much of which comes from organizations like the Uniform Plumbing Code publishers, and we repipe to current standards.

When to Call a Pro

Call us when repairs keep stacking up or pressure has fallen across the house. Planning a repipe beats reacting to leak after leak.

If you want to confirm the cause first, our leak and pressure diagnosis can pinpoint how widespread the problem really is.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my whole house needs repiping?

Recurring leaks in different spots, low pressure throughout, and old corroding pipe are the main signs. We assess the system and recommend repiping only when it is genuinely warranted.

How long does a repipe take?

It depends on the home's size and layout — often a few days. We plan the work to keep your water on as much as possible and minimize disruption.

Will you repair the walls afterward?

We keep openings small and coordinate finish repair. We will explain exactly what the job involves before we start.

Is repiping worth it versus repeated repairs?

If pipe is failing throughout, yes. The cost of endless repairs and water damage usually exceeds a planned repipe that solves it once.

What pipe do you use for repiping?

Modern, code-approved materials suited to your home. We will explain the choice and why it fits your situation.
