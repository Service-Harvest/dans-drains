Appliances We Hook Up

We connect the water side of appliances: dishwashers, refrigerator ice makers and water dispensers, washing machines, and similar. Each needs a proper supply connection and, in some cases, a drain.

For a laundry setup specifically, our laundry room plumbing work covers the valves and drain too.

Why Proper Hookup Matters

A loose or poorly connected appliance line is a slow leak waiting to happen — and appliances often sit against a wall or under a counter where a leak hides for a long time.

Doing the connection right, with a good shutoff, prevents exactly that.

How We Hook Up an Appliance

We connect the supply line with a proper valve, secure the fittings, and test for leaks. Where a drain is involved, we make sure it is connected and flowing. Then we confirm the appliance runs and holds water without dripping.

We also make sure there is an accessible shutoff so future service is easy.

Appliance Connections in Local Homes

In older kitchens and laundry areas here, the existing valves are sometimes aged or missing entirely. We add or update the shutoff so your new appliance is safe and serviceable.

Efficient appliances also save water, as ENERGY STAR washer information describes, and we connect them cleanly.

When to Call a Pro

Call us when you are installing a new dishwasher, ice maker, or washer and want the water connection done safely. A proper hookup prevents the slow leaks that damage floors and cabinets.

If a line has already leaked, we can check for hidden moisture damage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you connect my new dishwasher?

Yes. We connect the water supply and drain, add or check the shutoff, and test for leaks so it runs safely.

Do you hook up refrigerator ice makers?

Yes. We run or connect the water line to the fridge with a proper valve and confirm it holds without leaking.

Why does my washing machine need special valves?

Washer supply valves let you shut off the water when the machine is not in use, which reduces the risk of a burst hose flooding the room.

Can a poorly connected appliance cause a leak?

Yes, and often a hidden one, since appliances sit against walls or under counters. A proper connection with a good shutoff prevents it.

Do you add a shutoff if there isn't one?

Yes. We make sure there is an accessible shutoff so the appliance is safe and easy to service later.
