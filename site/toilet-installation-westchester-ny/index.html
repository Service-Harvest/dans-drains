Why a Proper Install Matters

A toilet that is not set right will rock, leak at the base, or run. Done properly, it seals to the floor, connects cleanly to the supply, and flushes reliably for years.

Getting the wax-ring seal right is the detail that prevents the slow base leaks behind many later toilet repairs.

How We Install a Toilet

We set a fresh seal, level the toilet to the floor, connect the supply, and test the flush and base for leaks. We haul away the old unit and leave the bathroom clean.

If your floor or flange is damaged, we address that first so the new toilet sits on a solid base.

Choosing the Right Toilet

Modern toilets vary in height, flush type, and water use. A comfort-height bowl and an efficient flush are popular upgrades. We are glad to install a model you have chosen or advise on options.

Efficient models save water without sacrificing performance, in line with EPA WaterSense guidance.

Toilet Upgrades in Local Homes

Many older bathrooms in this area still have original, water-hungry toilets. Swapping to an efficient model during a bathroom update is an easy win for both water use and reliability.

If you are remodeling, we coordinate the toilet with the rest of the bathroom plumbing work.

When to Call a Pro

Call us when your toilet is cracked, chronically leaking, or you simply want an upgrade. A clean, level install prevents the wobble and leaks of a rushed job.

Buying the toilet yourself? We are glad to install a unit you have already picked out.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you install a toilet I bought?

Yes. Bring us the model you chose and we will set it level, seal it, and leak-test it.

How long does a toilet installation take?

A standard replacement is usually quick — often under an hour or two — unless the floor or flange needs repair first.

Should I upgrade to a low-flow toilet?

Modern efficient toilets save water while flushing well. If your current toilet is old and water-hungry, an upgrade usually pays off.

What if my floor is damaged under the old toilet?

We check the flange and floor when we remove the old unit and address any damage so the new toilet sits on a solid, leak-free base.

Do you remove the old toilet?

Yes. We haul away the old unit and leave the bathroom clean.
