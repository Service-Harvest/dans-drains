What Our Bathroom Plumbing Covers

Whether you are refreshing a single bathroom or reworking the layout, we handle the plumbing that makes it work: moving or adding supply and drain lines, connecting fixtures, and pressure-testing everything before the walls close up.

That includes full bathroom remodel plumbing as well as individual upgrades like a new shower and valve installation or a replacement bathtub set and connected properly.

Planning the Plumbing Before You Build

The best time to get plumbing right is before the tile goes up. Moving a drain or supply line is far easier with the walls open, so we like to be part of the conversation early.

We will look at your layout, flag anything that is harder than it looks (an old cast-iron drain, a joist in the wrong place), and give you an honest sense of what your plan will take. That saves surprises halfway through the job.

Fixtures We Install and Connect

A bathroom is a collection of fixtures that all need solid plumbing behind them. We install and connect toilets — including setting and sealing a new toilet — sinks and vanities, showers with a fresh shower valve for steady temperature, tubs, and faucets, and we make sure the drains and vents are right so nothing gurgles or smells later.

If your project also touches the vanity plumbing, a new bathroom faucet fitted and sealed is a small detail that makes a big difference in the finished look.

Remodeling Older Westchester Bathrooms

Many bathrooms in this area sit in homes that are decades old, with original drain lines and supply pipes that may be a mix of materials. Opening a wall sometimes reveals aging plumbing that is worth updating while it is accessible.

We will tell you when that is genuinely worth doing and when it is fine to leave alone. For ideas on planning a remodel, the This Old House bathroom remodeling guides are a helpful starting point, and we handle the plumbing that turns those ideas into reality.

When to Bring in a Plumber for Your Remodel

Bring us in as soon as your plans involve moving a fixture, adding a bathroom, or updating old pipe. Getting the plumbing planned early keeps the whole project on schedule.

Even for a smaller refresh, a plumber's eye on the rough-in pays off. Take a look at everything else we can handle on the project if your remodel goes beyond the bathroom.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you do the whole remodel or just the plumbing?

We handle the plumbing side — rough-in, fixture connections, and drains. We work alongside your contractor or on the plumbing scope directly, and we are glad to coordinate timing.

Can you move a toilet or shower to a new spot?

Often, yes, though it depends on where the drains and joists are. We will look at your layout and tell you honestly what is straightforward and what is more involved.

Should I update old pipes during a remodel?

If a wall is already open and the pipe is aging, it is usually the cheapest time to update it. We will tell you when it is worth doing and when your existing pipe is fine to leave.

Will you pressure-test the new plumbing?

Yes. We test supply and drain connections before the walls close up, so a hidden leak does not turn into a problem behind your new tile.

Can you install the fixtures I bought myself?

In most cases, yes. Bring us the toilet, faucet, or fixtures you picked and we will install and connect them properly.
